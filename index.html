Large Projects:

Node Strike (19 Years Old)

This is a video that explains the gameplay of Node Strike. I submitted it along with the game to IGF 2026.

For Node Strike, I developed a procedural puzzle generation algorithm. I wanted to make a game with infinite replayability and time pressure to solve puzzles. This required inventing a brand new type of puzzle and an algorithm to generate them. Using graph theory, I built a unique puzzle-combat system that tests the player’s ability to think critically under pressure.

Blood on a Bird's Foot (새발의피) (20 Years Old)

Blood on a Bird's Foot is a short film animated using Blender and Unreal Engine in collaboration with 8k Productions.

For Blood on a Bird's Foot, I explored social injustice in South Korea through a fictional story the focuses on one riot police officer during a war. I experimented with unique animation styles, blending cel shading with painting-like texture overlays. For larger shots I developed a procedural city generation algorithm and built simulations for the riot scenes.

The Inner Demons (17 Years Old)

For The Inner Demons, I wanted to blur the lines between video games and cinema. By using a unique room-based control scheme, my camera system can film the game with cinematic angles. The result is a tense movie-like experience that feels distinct from any other horror game.

Terminal V Tech Demo (17 Years Old)

For Terminal V, my goal was to experiment with alternative player movement systems. By using the mouse to control the character’s position (rather than WASD to control velocity), players get to experience what it’s like to be a speedster with almost no limits, something that games have historically struggled to convey.

The Reflection (16 Years Old)

For The Reflection, I tried to explore spaces that wouldn’t be possible in real life. I settled on a retroreflective space that mirrors the geometry across a plane. This allows for interesting combat and platforming scenarios that would not be possible in a traditional game space.
